Privacy & Data Use

PrivacyLens processes all form data locally in your browser. Data you paste is never uploaded or shared.

  • 100% local processing with no backend or cloud.
  • No AI APIs or external services are used.
  • No analytics or trackers included.
  • State is not stored beyond localStorage and can be cleared.
  • Third-party resources are limited to safe, open CDNs for UI only.

Cookie Policy

PrivacyLens does not intentionally use cookies for advertising or tracking.

Browser localStorage may be used for storing app state, which is separate from cookies.

Hosting/CDN providers may have infrastructure behaviors outside the control of PrivacyLens.
